About King Welfare Foundation

Humanity First — Serving Humanity Is Our Mission

Organization: King Welfare Foundation

Founder: Habib Ullah

Location: Loralai, Balochistan, Pakistan

Our mission is to help orphan children, poor families, widows and persons with disabilities through food, education, healthcare and essential support.

We appeal to kind-hearted people in Pakistan and around the world to join us in serving humanity for the sake of Allah.
