Our Mission

We started King Welfare Foundation with the aim of helping orphan children, persons with disabilities, poor families and people in need.

Our mission is based on humanity, kindness and the desire to serve people for the sake of Allah.

Our Promise and Transparency

We are not a government organization or a registered foundation at present. This is a humanitarian initiative started with sincerity and the intention of helping people.

We will make every effort to deliver every donated rupee to deserving people. We will maintain records of donations and expenses.

We will share updates and videos of our welfare activities on our WhatsApp Channel, with the permission and dignity of the people receiving help.

We will work according to our ability and hope to take this mission from Pakistan to the whole world, InshaAllah.

As our mission grows, we hope to complete the necessary legal registration and establish an official foundation bank account.
