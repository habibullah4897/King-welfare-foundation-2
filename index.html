Transparency, Receipts and Proof of Donations

We are committed to transparency and responsible use of every donation.

For every donation received, we will make the available receipt, payment confirmation or other verifiable proof of receipt and expenditure available through our official WhatsApp Channel.

We will regularly share updates about donations received, funds spent and welfare activities carried out.

We will also share pictures and videos of our charitable activities where appropriate, while respecting the dignity, privacy and consent of the people receiving assistance.

Our goal is to make sure that every donor can follow how their contribution is being used.
